The house

Measured once. Dressed for every occasion after.

Nairobi Bespoke Atelier crafts garments around you — your frame, your taste, your occasion. From a first consultation in our CBD atelier, every piece is cut and finished to your exact measurements.

We work in fine British, Scottish and Italian fabrics, and we treat the smallest details — a lapel line, a cuff, a lining — as seriously as the silhouette itself.
